My friend Brad Pettit is playing with ChatGPT's image drawing capability, and had a brilliant idea, feed it the lyrics to a song and see what it comes back with. He tried [Low Spark of High-Heeled Boys](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vDGorIWYz-A) by Traffic, a great song, and [the image](https://www.facebook.com/bradpettit/posts/pfbid0m7TE3T5LJVr9Z5QznWdtWPS9bJxrXKnw1NX3UCeUtpqcg9Sg6SiNphqk7Cg7dKCel) was really cool.

This game me the idea to try it with a few of my favorite songs.
