![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/11/30/goodhumortruck.png)An advantage of using an outliner to edit a blog, is that you can easily split one post into several because they're edited in one document. Or move a paragraph from one story to another with drag and drop. In a page-oriented editor, you get to focus on the writing for one page, but navigating outside that post is more jarring, a writer more easily loses the train of thought. I've been [writing](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/31/outline.png) in an outliner almost exclusively for decades, only now considering the writing world outside. Learning a lot.
